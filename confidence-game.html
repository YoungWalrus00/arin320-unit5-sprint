The narrator reads each question. The host reads the correct answer and calls the score. Voices come from the browser’s speech engine, and Edge has the most natural ones. Two Double Check tiles hide on the board, and the game ends with the Final Call wager round.

HOW TO RUN IT

  1. Share this screen. The team in control picks a category and a value.
  2. The narrator reads the question. Either team can answer out loud. You judge it.
  3. Select Reveal. The host reads the answer. Award the points, or mark a team wrong and let the other team try.
  4. The team that answers right picks next. Made a mistake? Undo takes back the last score change.

Keys: Space reveal · 1/2 team right · Q/W team wrong · 0 nobody · R read again

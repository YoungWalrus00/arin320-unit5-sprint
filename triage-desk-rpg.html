BEFORE YOU CLOCK IN

STEP 1 · START THE LAB NOW

The AI Lab uses AWS, and the lab takes a few minutes to start. Start it first.

  1. Open the AWS Academy Learner Lab from LEO, the same way you did in Unit 3.
  2. Select Start Lab. The dot next to AWS turns yellow, then green.
  3. Don’t wait for green. Pick your character and start Ward 1.

Never select Reset. Use Chrome or Firefox on a laptop, not a phone.

STEP 2 · PICK A CLASS

Every class can earn the same XP. Classes start with different stats, and stats change how the night’s fights go. You’ll raise them every time you level up.

Your instructor puts you in a team of 3. Everyone plays on their own laptop, and you talk it out together.

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Press Enter or E to go in or talk. Or just click a door.
